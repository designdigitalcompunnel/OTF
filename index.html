04 / ABOUT OTF

A free internet helps people access information, communicate, and exercise human rights online. We support the technology that makes it possible.

ACCESS. SECURITY. FREEDOM.
THE WHY

Our mission

Open Technology Fund is an independent nonprofit advancing internet freedom in repressive environments and supporting human rights online.

THE HOW

Our approach

We support research, development, implementation, and maintenance of technologies for secure and uncensored internet access.
